Reel 02 The Arms

It started with a coat of arms.

The Corso family arms: a red field, an armored fist, a bundle of arrows, and roses.

One arrow snaps. A bundle doesn't.

It's the oldest lesson there is, and it's the whole idea behind this team. A father and two sons, bound together, pointed the same way.

So we redrew it for a race car.

The gauntlet became the driver's hand. The arrows became speed. The rose stayed, because it's still the family's.
